About Us

LifeCare Med Swiss, acting as CH-Rep, manufacturer, and importer, is headquartered in Zug, Switzerland. We are a strategic partner for distributors, importers, dealers, and healthcare professionals worldwide — with a strong focus on quality, efficiency, competitive pricing, and regulatory responsibility to ensure optimal global market access. Through our RegCheck Service, we also support international companies in meeting all regulatory requirements to bring their medical devices to market.
