About Us

LifeCare Med Swiss, acting as CH-Rep, manufacturer, and importer, is headquartered in Zug, Switzerland. We are a strategic partner for distributors, importers, dealers, and healthcare professionals worldwide — with a strong focus on quality, efficiency, competitive pricing, and regulatory responsibility to ensure optimal global market access.
